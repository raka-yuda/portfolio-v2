## Behind the scene

WatchStock project start from my impulsive thought to learn how to use ai agentic code and learn investment at the same time. For the tech side i’m curious with how everyone use ai to create a product. Either for build a commercial product or just to build an apps for them self. And beside that i’m curious as well when i doomscrolling at twitter someone caught my eye, so his timeline is always share what called watchlist for stock everyday. And i just wondering with myself why not i built for myself an app with all of computatinal prediction for the same purpose but with all my financial risk that i have.
