## The need

I run a handful of personal services on a homelab. When one of them gets sluggish, I want to know *while I'm working*, not after I've already lost an hour wondering why my own blog feels slow.

## What it shows

A single dot in the macOS menu bar. Green for healthy, amber for sluggish, red for unreachable. Clicking the dot opens a small popover with a sparkline per service.

## Constraints I gave myself

- **No backend.** Everything runs on-device.
- **Under 20MB of RAM.** Currently holding at 11.
- **No analytics.** I don't need to know how anyone uses it.
